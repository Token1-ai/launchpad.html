💡 How Launch works
1
Anyone can buy or sell — no pass needed. Just connect a wallet with some BNB.
2
Everyone gets 1 free token launch — no pass needed. After that: Silver Pass gives up to 7 per week, Gold Pass up to 3 every day.
3
Every token starts on a bonding curve — a formula, not a person, sets the price. The more people buy, the higher the price climbs. Selling brings it back down.
4
Every trade has an 11% fee: 10% goes straight to the token's creator, 1% to the platform. Paid automatically, every single trade.
5
Once a token raises enough BNB, it "graduates": liquidity moves to PancakeSwap automatically and the LP is burned forever — nobody, not even the creator, can pull the liquidity out. That's the anti-rug guarantee.
