💡 How Launch works
1
Anyone can buy or sell — no pass needed. Just connect a wallet with some BNB.
2
Creating a new token requires a Silver or Gold Pass. Silver: up to 7 tokens per week. Gold: up to 3 tokens every day.
3
Every token starts on a bonding curve — a formula, not a person, sets the price. The more people buy, the higher the price climbs. Selling brings it back down.
4
Every trade has an 11% fee: 10% goes straight to the token's creator, 1% to the platform. Paid automatically, every single trade.
5
Once a token raises enough BNB, it "graduates": liquidity moves to PancakeSwap automatically and the LP is burned forever — nobody, not even the creator, can pull the liquidity out. That's the anti-rug guarantee.

💡 How List & Trade works
1
List a token you already have — your own project token, or one created on our Launch tab.
2
Choose the quote currency: BNB, USDT, or USDC. You deposit both your token and the quote currency to seed the market.
3
🔒 Locked means the liquidity stays forever — nobody can withdraw it. This builds trust with buyers. ⚠️ Removable means you can withdraw it later, but buyers take on more risk.
4
Anyone can buy or sell, no pass required. Listing a token requires a Silver or Gold Pass, same limits as Launch.
5
Every trade has a 1% fee: 0.5% to the lister automatically, 0.5% to the platform.
